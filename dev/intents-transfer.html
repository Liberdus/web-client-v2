Liberdus wallet — Intents transfer

Phase 4: intents-transfer.js against mainnet. A transfer settles inside intents.near between two Liberdus accounts — no chain fee, no bridge, no NEAR. The relayer submits it and pays the gas.

Simulate is read-only. Publish is not. Simulating asks the verifier what the transfer would do and changes nothing. Publishing sends it for execution and moves real funds between real accounts. There is no undo.
